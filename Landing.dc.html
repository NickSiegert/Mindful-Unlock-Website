The unlock is the whole problem

Most screen-time tools step in after you're already fifteen minutes into an app. Mindful Unlock changes what happens the moment you unlock — before the drift starts, not after.

Three ways it gets in the way — on purpose

01 — Keep the home screen small

Only a handful of apps live on your home screen. Want something else? Type its name.

02 — Set blocking groups

Group apps, then decide their access: a daily budget, an open limit, a time window, or weekends only.

03 — Make it deliberate

Need through a block? Wait it out, scan a code, or walk 50 steps. Never instant.

Deliberate, not instant

A block that lifts with one tap isn't a block. Choose how yours lifts: a delayed wait, a QR code, or a walk of set steps.

Rules, not willpower

Blocking groups hold whichever apps tempt you most — even "Shorts only" inside YouTube. Each group gets its own rule.
