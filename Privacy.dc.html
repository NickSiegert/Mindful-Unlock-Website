Privacy policy

Mindful Unlock · Effective date: August 11, 2026

In short: Mindful Unlock does not have a server, does not collect analytics, and does not transmit your data anywhere. Everything the app needs to function — your blocking rules, app usage, and settings — is stored only on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app can see, why, and what happens to it.

1. Who this policy covers

Mindful Unlock is developed by Lolium Labs. This policy applies to the Mindful Unlock Android app available on Google Play.

2. Data we do not collect

Mindful Unlock has no backend server and no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not collect, receive, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (there is no account or sign-in)
  • Which apps you use, for how long, or when — this stays on your device
  • Your blocking group configuration, favorites, or app usage history
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, or any other personal files
  • Analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers

3. What the app accesses, and why

Mindful Unlock requests several Android permissions. Every one exists to make the app's core feature — an app-blocking home screen replacement — work. None of the data these permissions expose ever leaves your device.

Permission What it's used for
Default launcher (Home role) Mindful Unlock replaces your home screen so it can require typing an app's name before opening it.
Usage access Lets the app see which app is currently in the foreground, so blocking rules apply even if an app is opened from a notification, Recents, or a link — not just from Mindful Unlock's own search. Processed and stored locally only.
Display over other apps Lets the app automatically return you to the home screen when a time limit or schedule blocks an app you're currently using.
Notifications Required by Android to show the silent, ongoing "monitoring active" notification that a background service is required to display.
Camera (optional feature) Only used if you enable "Scan to pause," which reads a QR code you printed yourself to temporarily lift a block. The camera feed is processed on-device to decode the code and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
Physical activity / step counter (optional feature) Only used if you enable "Steps to pause," which counts steps walked to temporarily lift a block. Step counts are read from the device sensor and are not stored beyond the current session.
Accessibility service (optional feature) Only requested if you enable optional short-form video blocking (e.g. YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels). This lets the app recognize when a targeted app is showing a short-form video feed, using on-screen element patterns, so it can return you to the previous screen. It does not read or process any other content, and nothing it observes is collected, logged remotely, or transmitted off your device. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, and the permission is never requested unless you choose to enable this feature.
Uninstall shortcut Used only if you tap "App info" or "Uninstall" from the long-press menu on an app — this opens Android's own system screens and does not transmit anything.

4. Where your data lives

All app data — blocking groups, favorites, usage and open-count history, onboarding progress, and your subscription status — is stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore. Nothing is synced to a cloud service or backed up to our servers, because we don't operate any. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it.

5. Subscriptions and payment

Premium subscriptions are purchased and processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details — Google handles billing, receipts, and subscription management. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google Play handles this information. The app only stores locally whether your subscription is currently active, which it checks with Google Play Billing directly.

6. Sending us feedback

If you use the "Send feedback" option in Settings, the app opens your own email app with a pre-filled message addressed to support@loliumlabs.com, including your app version, Android version, and device model to help with troubleshooting. This is sent only if and when you choose to send that email yourself — the app does not transmit anything automatically. You can review and edit the message, including removing the diagnostic information, before sending. Any email you send us is handled like ordinary correspondence and is not shared with third parties.

7. Children's privacy

Mindful Unlock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age — the app simply doesn't collect personal information at all.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and post the revised version at this same address. Since the app doesn't collect contact information, we won't be able to notify you directly — please check back here if you'd like to review the current version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or how Mindful Unlock handles data can be sent to support@loliumlabs.com.
